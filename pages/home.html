The method

The part other coaches skip

Most programs assume your body is a stock configuration. Yours isn't. It has old ankle sprains, a shoulder that clicks, and fifteen years of compensations stacked on top. We start there.

01

Assess

We test how you actually move — joint by joint, under load — before a single set gets written. Your coach should not be improvising.

02

Rebuild

Positions, tissue tolerance, and the joints that quit on you. It's unglamorous work, and it's the reason the heavy work stops hurting.

03

Load

Then we train hard. Heavy, fast, competitive, measured. Rehab was never the destination — getting back on the field was.
